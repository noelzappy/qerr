# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are
not accepted, in the repository or in any space connected to it.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability** opens a private channel to the maintainers; say in the title that it is a conduct report).
Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors who break it repeatedly or seriously.
