# Photo credits

One row for every photo in this folder. The photos themselves are not committed (git ignores everything here except this
file and `README.md`); this file is, so the licences stay on record.

Before you add a photo, check that its licence allows **commercial use** (a brand post is commercial). "Free for personal
use" is not enough. See "The photo rules" in `docs/design.md`.

What to record:

- **File**: the name in this folder, for example `market-street.jpg`.
- **Source**: the page you got it from (a stock site's photo page, or "own photo" with who took it).
- **Author**: the photographer or the account that published it, if known.
- **Licence**: its name, and that it allows commercial use.
- **Date**: when you downloaded or received it.
- **People**: none, hands only, a distant crowd, or identifiable faces (and whether consent is recorded).
- **What is in it**: a short description, including anything to watch for: brand names, readable signs, children, money
  of another currency.
- **Used**: which posts use it, or "not used".
